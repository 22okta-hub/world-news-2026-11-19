An offline [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261119) entry for **Sum up 19 November's world news in one image**.

The poster was drawn offline as an illustrated news page with 21 selected stories across world regions. Its three lead stories cover the Shenzhen meeting of Trump, Xi, and Putin, Anthropic's record IPO, and the ICJ ruling on Myanmar and the Rohingya. Illustrated secondary stories and five brief sections cover climate, security, sport, culture, technology, health, the economy and weather.

The illustrations are geometric and symbolic; no news photographs, logos, official art, or recognizable political likenesses are used. Reporting facts are listed in [sources.md](sources.md).

## Run

The program uses only Node's standard library. It copies the bundled PNG bytes to the required `/output/entry.png` path.

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
